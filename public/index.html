Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal to download YouTube videos to MP4?

Downloading YouTube videos may be subject to YouTube's Terms of Service. StreamSave is intended for downloading videos you have permission to download — such as your own uploads, Creative Commons content, or videos explicitly permitted by their creator. Always respect copyright law.

How do I convert YouTube to MP4 in 1080p?

Paste your YouTube link into StreamSave. Once the video loads, you'll see quality options including 1080p60, 1080p, 720p60 and more. Click the 1080p pill to download that specific quality as an MP4 file.

Can I download a full YouTube playlist?

Currently StreamSave downloads one video at a time. Paste the URL of a specific video rather than the playlist URL for best results.

Why is a quality option greyed out or missing?

Quality options depend entirely on what the original uploader made available on YouTube. If a specific quality like 4K is missing, the video was not uploaded in that resolution. Downloading 1080p+ also requires ffmpeg to be installed on the server for audio/video merging.

What is the fastest YouTube video downloader?

StreamSave streams directly from YouTube's servers to your browser in real-time. Download speed depends on your internet connection and YouTube's delivery speed — no intermediate re-upload or transcoding delay.

Does StreamSave work on mobile?

Yes. StreamSave is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers including Safari (iPhone/iPad) and Chrome (Android). The interface adapts to smaller screens automatically.
